# Score-Margin Distribution Model — Shadow Experiment

*Generated: 2026-08-06*

## Motivation

Standard Elo + Platt predicts win probability indirectly (margin→logistic scale→prob). 
A score-margin distribution model predicts the full distribution of 
home_score - away_score as Normal(μ, σ²), then derives win probability as P(margin > 0) = Φ(μ/σ).

This approach naturally captures uncertainty, avoids Platt distortion at extremes, 
and produces blowout probabilities and credible intervals.
